*"Brown-eyed Alavandor. You have the look of your grand uncle."*

*"You appear as one I have been expecting, but this is a place of
shadow, with deceit woven into the few lighted places to be found. How
can I be sure of who you are?"*

*In life, I was not a plain-speaking man, and the discipline of my Order
was to say little, and to treat words like weapons, which you will agree
they are. In this...place...I have become a more extreme version of
myself; my mind and tongue shackled by the dark, my body
nigh-on-indestructible. Yet, I will gladly exchange news and riddles on
small or common matters. But, on issues where our fates are tightly
intertwined, I can have meaningful discourse only with those that best
me in combat, and vow to end this...atrocity...of being...*

### Superficial Information

#### Hoetmer

*The kind friar, I liked him. He and I met several times a long time
ago. He is overdue, in fact, having told me at our last parting that he
would share some wonderful news with me when the time is right. We were
alike, with regard to our riddles, but of our bodies, completely
opposite. For he was a soul without a body, and I body without a soul.*

#### Loki

*The silver-haired Shadowvar. She is dangerous, most dangerous thing in
these woods. She has been here long time, longer even then Anavex. She
is first among her master's servants. This I know, as I've battled many
such as her. She could destroy me, but chooses not to. She is cunning,
patient, so so patient. She is also the gap in her master's armour. More
I could say...*

#### Palus

*Much have I to say on the matter of the witch over yon (and he points
to the east-south-east, and noting a puzzled expression on your faces
responds...) ah...I see that you are yet unfamiliar with your other
adversary. She is three things in three places, but more I cannot yet
say..."*

#### Alvund Alavandor

*Two men I knew who shared a name, one borrowed it to hide, the other
hid it to borrow. I was fond only of one these men, and it was not the
one whose eyes you look through. That one I delivered as I vowed, from
my home in Luthcheq, to his final destination.*

#### Gelzamael

*Winged master of the sunless sky, terror of all the forest. Masterless,
but not without friends and sometimes allies. It cannot destroy me. It
has tried, and I have enjoyed its attempts. It has not visited me for a
long time. I think it tired of having its nose bloodied by me. Gelzamael
is spiteful, and has a cruel memory. I don't think it would destroy me
if it could. It could send henchmen to do for me, but it doesn't.*

#### Shevah

*Of all the wicked things in this forest, Shevah is chief. For she is
not of the forest but of the bog, and she is a sergeant for the witch,
doing her bidding directly. Shevah World Walker some cretins call her,
for she attends her queen in all her courts.*

#### The Iron Hole

*Of course you seek the way out, the easiest way out of many. My brother
found the way out many years ago, for this was his trade. He traversed
himself to be sure of the safety of th passage, then came back for the
rest of us. Before we were separated by martial necessity, he told me
how to use the passage, for unlike many passages, this one does not want
to be found, and a marching riddle there is to use it.*

*The Iron Mine is indeed the way out. It is an old and stable passage.
But it is hidden. The mine entrance is here (he indicates on your map),
and will likely be guarded by the Shadowvar or their agents; they must
guess that you will attempt to escape that way, now that you've left the
other passages behind you.\
\
You will descend into the mine, into darkness beneath darkness. There
too, will be adversaries and hazards. You'll need your wits and blades
sharp and ready. You will come before long to the bottom of the mine,
for it is not very deep. There is an unnatural pillar at the bottom, you
cannot miss it, or so I am told. You must walk about it counterclockwise
three times, and on the third, you will be on the other side.*

#### The Original Alavaxi

*Ha, like so many others in this tale, our real name was not meant to be
shared, for like those that became Alavandors, my name had deeds
attached to it; great for some were these deeds, and for others, our
enemies, deeds that demanded retribution. But Alavaxi will do, and my
brother will no doubt have a keener memory for what happened before we
came here, and why. But the Alavaxi we became were strong, veterans of
long campaigns of war with our enemies, and long campaigns of commerce
with our friends.*

### Key Information

#### Loki

*The Shadowvar is a Curator, a seeker of Ancient Netherese artefacts.
I've met some such as her, as Netheril extended to the very edge of The
Ancient Empires in her day. Indeed, Netherese crypts and deep forgotten
places can be found beneath Luthcheq, and their existence drew the
Netherese to us.\
The Curators are of original stock, born in Netheril before it's return
to our world. In fact, she will remember a time when she was not a
creature washed in shadow, but an educated and important woman in the
court of an empire millennia old. This memory is a pain to those that
returned. And so they turn to Shar, and forget, and embrace their new
reality. But the Curators are marked by fate; theirs is the terrible
duty to remember, and to be filled with the memories of a mightier
empire where the light of Amaunator and the grace of all their ancient
gods comingled with their tremendous power and learning of to create the
greatest civilization in the history of men, perhaps of all Faerun.*

#### Personal --- the Luthcheq backstory and Alvund

*You should ask my brother, because he was always the bright one; good
at finding things, deals, people, opportunities, mischief. Ask him about
the Old Patriarch, Alvund Abranneth. I wish my memory was clearer, but
it is not. Shadow has crept into my mind, and I eagerly await my
delivery from it. What I remember of the Old Patriarch was that he was
kind, and kept his word. And we did our best to emulate that. I remember
that he was caught up in a terrible struggle with Netheril, a foe that
was known to us, even in distant Luthcheq. He and his friend, General
Arandal. I wish I could remember his name and order...they advised us on
the strengths and weaknesses of this enemy, and we prevailed. We repaid
them decades latter, answering the call to assist them in their time of
struggle. Aravex will remember more, you should ask him...*

#### The Alavaxi Summons

*You have already witnessed many of the strengths of my order, which I
will not name here. We trained to rely on nothing but ourselves; our
wits, our speed, our courage, our bodies. This was useful in our
struggle with the sorcerers of the Old Empire, and their vile distant
neighbours to the north, whom we were oft-sought out to oppose in many
centuries in many places of Faerun.*

*Though the Old Patriarch and his friend the General had waged war for
decades, they needed our skills to prevail. They hoped they could outrun
the Shadow, or hide from it, but you can see looking around, neither
option is viable for long. Eventually, you face the Shadow, and you must
prepare for that day.*

*Do you know, the old Friar once told me, many years after my term here
began, that the younger men of my order had done their duty, and that
the seed of our discipline had grown, those were his words, or maybe
they were the words of the Old Matriarch...that was a confusing matter
before my mind was wreathed in shadow...but it brought me great pride at
the time. Younglings were mastering our discipline, I was told. But then
it came to ashes and ruin, and I learned that a husk without a soul can
still find a new depth of sorrow.*

#### Palus

*I have never met the Contessa of the Sorrow Bog, but I have met many of
her minions, and many has she sent to talk with me, and get information.
I gave them little, but gained much in these exchanges. The witch was
transformed in 1441, a date I mark only because others have told it to
me. She made a deal with the Netherese, to do whatever it is she did to
Thistle Ridge and the people there. I don't know the details of that,
but I know that her Court now extends to this...place...and that she
spends most of her time here, in the black copy of her Court. She is
powerful now, though bound somehow, I get the impression. This place
does make effective prisons, does it not? She has never been to talk to
me, but has sent so many messengers. I have invited her to talk with me,
but she has never availed herself of the offer. Her cruelest captain in
Sheva World Walker. Sheva can go between all three of her Courts,
carrying out sundry errands. I have refrained from destroying Sheva,
wondering if there might be some use for it, though I'm not sure what
that might be.*

*The Court of the Sorrow Bog also extends into the Fey, I have been
told. Sheva will boast of it if given the chance. Sheva has told me that
few know this secret, and that the Contessa is getting close to some
immortal state of being, her court drawing power from three realms.*

#### Tenebrius Ostium

*I have never seen these things, but I have heard of them. They are
traps, similar to mine, but different in important ways. They create a
space that exists in two places at once, according to Sheva; both here
and home. Their main purpose I have surmised; they provide power for the
Contessa of the Sorrow Bog, and are a part of her foothold here in
Shadow. They were woven into the deal with the Netherese, though the
Contessa was fooled by them. Again, according to Sheva, Palus did not
get what she wanted out of the deal. This has become clear to her over
the last couple of years, I think, though time is difficult for me to
track.*

*I think it was only last summer that one of her daughters and a grand
daughter came to me, to ask again about Netheril. They had done their
research and knew who I was, and how exactly I came to be here. And of
course, they knew the Old Patriarch's secrets, and had guessed why the
Netherese wanted him destroyed. Chiefly, they asked after the curse that
had been put on the two Sembian Families from Daerlun. I laughed at
them, and the foolishness of making friends with Shade and their
terrible deities. "I have seen daughters and grand daughters of the
Contessa" I said to them. "And I can see that you, witchling,
(addressing the younger of the two) are old enough to bear children.
Tell me, how many daughters do you have?" They departed in anger, and I
laughed to see them retreat so wounded.*

#### Aravex

*If the Friar thinks my brother is still alive, I am sure he is correct.
I can rest peacefully. Aravex will be happy to see you, if you grant him
these as token (he says, as he unwraps his wrists and hands over the
well-kept linens). Aravex knew much more about our mission, and our
adversaries. He was also a veteran of battling with the Shadow, and no
doubt could prove useful in preparing you for subsequent journeys here.
Tell him I am sorry I didn't get to instruct our students in Thistle
Ridge.*
